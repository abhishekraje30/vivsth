Set B, cut twice

Your calendar marks, drawn at two optical sizes so they hold up where they actually get used. Left is the drawing as it was; right is the small cut. Both are the same icon family.

The bottom strip of each column is blurred slightly — that is roughly what a 1.7px stroke does on a density-2.0 Android at 16px, where a hairline lands on one device pixel and either aliases or drops out. It is the cheapest honest simulation of the real device.
