The guest invitation and RSVP page

Vasant kaka is in Pune. He has never heard of Vivah Spot, will not install it, and has no account. A message arrives from Rutuja’s own number — the platform never messages a Guest — and he replies in about fifteen seconds.

Frames 2, 3 and 4 are live. Tap an answer and the reply block becomes the recorded answer in place — no redirect, no success screen, no account offer. The fold readout under each frame is measured at runtime with getBoundingClientRect(), not asserted. Frame 1’s chat chrome is a simulation of WhatsApp and is deliberately outside the token system; everything the platform controls inside the preview is drawn from DESIGN.md.

Frame 5 · the count

360 × 640, after Yes

Tapping Yes reveals {components.stepper-count}, already reading four — the figure Rutuja recorded for the Deshmukh household. He changes nothing and confirms, so the count costs one extra tap only when her number was already right. No and Not sure reveal nothing: nobody is asked how many of them are not coming. The centre value is keyboard-editable, not stepper-only; at 1 the decrement is disabled and says why.

Frame 4 · a larger phone

430 × 760, unanswered

430px is {spacing.phone-column}, the column’s cap, so this is the widest the page ever gets: above 430 the column centres on the ground rather than stretching. The card leaves its compact variant — names back to 1.2rem, more inset, roomier Function rows — while the reply block is byte-identical to frame 2.
