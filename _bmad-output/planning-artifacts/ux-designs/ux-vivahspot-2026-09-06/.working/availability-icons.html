Icons for the availability ring

Three sets, each drawn as inline SVG at a 2px stroke with round caps and joins. Shown large enough to judge, then at the real 16px inside the ring, then in the grid where they live.

The ring treatment — solid, thin, dashed — already carries the state on its own. The icon is the second signal, never the only one, so it can afford to be quiet. Greyscale underneath each set.
