The availability signal, where it actually lives

Both forms, in the two places the signal appears: a two-up results grid at 360px — the tight case — and a full-width list row, where there is room for anything. Three vendors, one in each state.

The copy never asserts on a Vendor's behalf: shows available, never available. The third state is not a rejection — it fires when there are no dates yet, or the Service has no duration, or the Subscription has lapsed — so nothing about it may read as a refusal. Greyscale strips underneath.

One ring, three treatments

Solid, thin, dashed — all three in {colors.muted}. The border WIDTH and STYLE separate them before colour or icon does anything; the Set B calendar is the second signal and the word is the third.
