Where the wedding actually lives

The invitation card on your home screen holds four facts. Rutuja's wedding has three functions, five services, twelve shortlisted vendors, a running total against ₹8L and a photographer who hasn't quoted yet. Here are four different rooms to keep that in.

All four are built from DESIGN.md tokens and obey the contrast rules the distillation caught me breaking — muted is never faded, every progress bar carries a text label because turmeric on gold-tint is 1.36:1, and undecided money always reads not yet estimated. One vendor is named in Devanagari in each so you can see Inknut doing its job in situ.

Tap the card, get everything. A single scroll holding the block, the functions, the services and the budget. One destination, nothing hidden, the wedding whole.
