What does "something went wrong" look like?

Vermillion #E01B33 means action — it is on every primary button in the product. So red cannot also mean danger, and DESIGN.md has no error colour at all. Three ways out, against the three failures that will actually happen.

All three state the failure in words — "nothing essential by colour alone" is the floor, not a treatment. What varies is whether colour helps at all. The cases are real: NFR 5.3 targets mid-range Androids on patchy mobile data, often at a venue or in a market, so Dattatray losing signal mid-quote is the normal case, not the edge.

Treatment 1

Words and shape

No new colour at all. Failure is carried by a heavy ink rule, a glyph and plain language. Hue does nothing, so nothing can be missed by not seeing hue.

The purist option, and the cheapest. Four colours stay four colours, and the accessibility floor is met by construction rather than by audit. The risk is real though: a heavy ink rule reads as emphasis, not alarm, and a vendor scanning fast on a cracked screen may simply not register that the quote failed.

Treatment 2

One new token — clay

A sixth colour, #8C2F1A, reserved for failure and destructive confirmation and used nowhere else. Dark, earthy, and unmistakably not vermillion.

The legible option. White on clay is 8.3:1 and it reads as trouble instantly, without argument. Two costs: it is a second red in a palette that already has one, so a red-blind eye sees two dark warm blocks — and the moment a sixth token exists, someone will use it for a "danger" that is really just emphasis. It needs a written rule, not just a value.

Treatment 3

Turmeric carries trouble

No new token. The celebration colour doubles as the attention colour — it already marks the stale-date nudge, which is the product's existing "this needs you" signal.

The one I'd argue against, and it is worth seeing why. Turmeric already means two things — celebration on the invitation card, and "we need something from you" on the nudge. Making it mean failure as well is a third job for one colour, and the failed quote ends up wearing the same gold as the finished wedding card. Scan the three panes quickly: this is the column where the failure reads least like a failure.
