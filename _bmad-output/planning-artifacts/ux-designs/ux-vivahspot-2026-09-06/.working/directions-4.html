Four directions for the Vivah Spot home screen

Same screen, same content, four different souls. Each is a complete personality — palette, type, density, corner language and motion — not a colour swap. The strip at the top of each is a different idea about what greets Rutuja when she opens the app.

The animations run on a loop so you can see them without tapping. Under each phone is the same strip in its first-run state, before she has told us anything. Hex values are in the stylesheet comments. Pick a direction, not a detail — everything inside it is still adjustable.

Marigold & Ink

Heirloom. Warm cream, letterpress serif, square corners, gold rule. Feels printed rather than rendered.

An empty card, waiting. She fills it by living in the app.

Why it works: the completion metaphor is one she already owns — nobody needs to be told an unfinished invitation means the wedding isn't sorted. Each new fact ink-writes onto the card; the gold foil sweeps once when it completes. What it costs: a card holds four facts, not fifteen, so everything else lives below the fold.
