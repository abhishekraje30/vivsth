The vendor portal at width

You're right — apps/vendor-web is a Next.js app deploying to vendor.vivahspot.com. NFR 5.2's "phone-first" sets the priority, not the ceiling: Dattatray reads an enquiry standing in someone else's mandap, and prices it properly at his desk that night. Both are real. Here are the same four surfaces at ≥1024px.

A persistent left rail replaces the tab bar above 768px — the five destinations are the same five, just always visible. Below 768px each of these collapses to the phone in direction-vendor-4.html. Notice what width does to the argument: the tension between "answer leads" and "prove the subscription is worth it" mostly dissolves here, because a right rail can hold the season's numbers without displacing the inbox. That tension is a phone problem, not a web one.
