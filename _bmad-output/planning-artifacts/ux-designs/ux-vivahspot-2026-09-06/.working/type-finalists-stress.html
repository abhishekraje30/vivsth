Rozha One vs Amita vs Inknut Antiqua

All three are beautiful on three names at 1.35rem. That is not where the decision lives. Below is every place Devanagari will actually appear in the product — a vendor's name in a list row, a line where both scripts share a row, a wall of Rules, a family's review, a filter chip — at the real sizes.

Read the Rules and Review blocks first, then the list row. If a face is hard work there, it is hard work in the parts of the app people spend their time in. The card is the smallest part of the job.

One family, both scripts, no seam and no rule about when to switch. Playfair is a high-contrast modern face and Inknut is a heavy old-style one — they are not close cousins, so a card carrying both will always read as two decisions. This reads as one. The cost is that you lose Playfair's elegance in the English, which is the face currently sitting on your live site.
