What this assumes
  • She arrives not knowing what a wedding needs, and the six tiles teach her.
  • She browses one category at a time and holds the wedding in her head.
  • The wedding itself — dates, functions, budget — lives somewhere behind “Planner”.
What it costs
  • Contradicts FR-68: “The Family never browses the catalog category by category.”
  • Contradicts FR-10: the Workspace is meant to be home, not a tab.
  • Availability can only be a per-vendor calendar — and UJ-1 says she never types a date into a filter.
  • The cross-service date matching — the thing no competitor does — has nowhere to live.
