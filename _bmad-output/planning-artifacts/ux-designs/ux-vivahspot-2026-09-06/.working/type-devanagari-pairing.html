Which Devanagari serif goes on the card

The same invitation card, in Kumkum & Turmeric, with the couple named ओंकार आणि स्नेहल and the venue शुभमंगल लॉन्स — both of which are user-entered content and can arrive in any script. Underneath each card, a vendor's Rules in Marathi, because that is where Devanagari appears at length and small sizes.

The English lines — the date, the labels, the progress count — stay in Playfair on every card, because the interface itself is English-only (prd.md:1259). What you are judging is whether the two faces look like one design or like two fonts sharing a box. The first card is the do-nothing case.
