Inbox and Calendar, one surface

The middle path isn't a toggle between two screens. It's two lenses on the same set of Enquiries — arranged by recency, or arranged by date. The switch changes the arrangement, not the destination.

What makes it one surface rather than two: selection carries across. Pick 27 November on the calendar, hit Inbox, and the list is filtered to that date with a chip saying so. Clear the chip and you're back to everything. The rail never moves — the nudge and the season's numbers are constant in both views, so the subscription argues for itself either way.
