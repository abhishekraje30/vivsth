One design rule established across all three: the unfavourable answer is never rendered in {colors.danger}. The platform does not judge these answers and cannot change them, so colouring "No" as a failure would be Vivah Spot taking a side — which is precisely what §7.2 excludes. It is a word in ink. Nothing else.
