His calendar at a real 360px

All three frames are exactly 360px wide with overflow hidden — nothing is scaled down to fit the page. The switch, the app bar and the padding are the same in each, so the only variable is what the calendar itself becomes.

Bars are {colors.vermillion} in both states now — turmeric measured 1.608:1 against the surface and could not carry the mark at all. One bar = an Agreement holds it. Two segments = Enquiries pending. No bar, struck number = he marked it unavailable.
